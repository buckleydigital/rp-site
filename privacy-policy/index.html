Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

ReclaimPower (ABN pending) ("we", "us", "our") operates reclaimpower.com.au. We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. What information we collect

We collect personal information you provide directly when you submit a match request, including:

  • Name, email address and phone number
  • Property postcode and ownership status
  • Existing solar setup and battery goals

We also collect non-personal information automatically, such as browser type, pages visited, referral source and IP address, via cookies and analytics tools (including Google Analytics).

2. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Match you with a CEC-accredited battery installer in your area
  • Contact you regarding your match and quote
  • Send relevant follow-up information you have consented to receive
  • Improve our website, tools and matching algorithms
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Disclosure to third parties

Your personal information is shared with:

  • Your matched installer only — the single CEC-accredited installer we match you with based on your postcode. They are contractually prohibited from sharing your information further or using it for marketing to other prospects.
  • Service providers — we use third-party services including form processing, analytics and email delivery. These parties process data on our behalf under data processing agreements.
  • Regulators — if required by law or court order.

4. Marketing communications

We may send you educational content about home batteries and solar by email if you have consented. You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us at hello@reclaimpower.com.au.

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience and track aggregate usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function correctly without them. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to track anonymised usage data.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. Data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure. Despite these measures, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Match enquiry data is typically retained for 3 years from the date of collection.

8. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Complain about a breach of your privacy rights
  • Opt out of direct marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@reclaimpower.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy, please contact us first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at reclaimpower.com.au/privacy-policy. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy enquiries: hello@reclaimpower.com.au

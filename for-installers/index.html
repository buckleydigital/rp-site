Partner Requirements

We only partner with installers who meet our standards.

Our homeowners trust our recommendation. We protect that trust by only partnering with installers who pass every item on this criteria list.

✓Current CEC Designer & Installer accreditation (individual, not just company)
✓Valid state electrical contractor licence, verified independently
✓Public liability insurance ≥ $5M (certificate of currency required)
✓Minimum 2 years operating as a battery installer in Australian market
✓No unresolved complaints with state electrical safety regulator
✓Ability to supply and apply the federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate
✓Compliant with AS/NZS 5139 battery installation standards
✓Commitment to obligation-free quoting and no high-pressure sales practices

FAQ

Common questions about home batteries in Australia

How much is the federal home battery rebate in 2026?
The federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program provides roughly 30% off the installed cost of an eligible battery. On a typical 10kWh system this saves $3,500–$4,800 upfront. The program is government-funded through to 2030, though the rebate structure changed on 1 May 2026 — earlier is better to lock in current terms.
Do I need solar panels to get a home battery?
No — but most Australians pair batteries with solar for the best financial return. If you already have solar, a battery lets you store your excess daytime generation and use it at night instead of paying peak grid rates. Without solar, a battery can still shift grid energy from off-peak to peak times to cut bills. We can match installers for solar-only, battery-only, or combined systems.
How much does a home battery cost in Australia in 2026?
A typical 10kWh home battery system costs $7,000–$9,500 installed after the federal rebate. Popular brands include Tesla Powerwall 3 ($13,000–$16,000 before rebate), BYD Battery-Box ($8,500–$11,000), Sungrow SBR ($7,500–$10,000), and Sigenergy. Your exact cost depends on brand, capacity, and your property's electrical requirements.
What does CEC-accredited mean and why does it matter?
CEC (Clean Energy Council) accreditation means the installer has completed approved training, complies with Australian installation standards, and is authorised to install systems that qualify for government rebates. You must use a CEC-accredited installer to access the federal rebate. Every installer in the ReclaimPower network holds current CEC accreditation — non-negotiable.

Genuinely free for homeowners. We earn a referral fee from the matched installer if and when you choose to proceed — but only then, and it never affects your quoted price. The guides, tools, and matching service cost you nothing with zero obligation, ever.
Why one installer instead of three competing quotes?
Because being bombarded by four competing salespeople is a terrible experience. We match you exclusively with one top-rated, vetted installer in your area. They get your enquiry exclusively — which means they're more invested in earning your business. If you're not happy after speaking to them, come back and we'll find you an alternative, no questions asked.
How long does a home battery installation take?
Most residential battery installations are completed in one day. The installer needs access to your switchboard and a suitable wall for the battery unit. Your matched installer will walk you through everything specific to your property during the quoting process.

Whether you want solar only, a home battery only, or both together, our matching process connects you with the right vetted installer in your area.

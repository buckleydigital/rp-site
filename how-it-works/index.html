How It Works

From your details to a quote in 24 hours.

No obligation, no four salespeople, no cowboys. Here's exactly what happens when you submit your details to ReclaimPower.

The Process

Four steps to your best battery quote.

Simple, transparent, and faster than any alternative.

1
Tell us about your home (2 minutes)

Answer five quick questions: whether you own your home, your solar status, your main battery goal, your postcode and your contact details. No electricity bill required at this stage.

What we collect
  • Property ownership status
  • Existing solar setup
  • Primary goal (bill savings, backup, export optimisation)
  • Postcode (to identify your area installer)
  • Name, email, phone
2
We identify your area's #1 installer

We match your postcode against our vetted network and identify the single best CEC-accredited installer for your area. We consider: installation volume in your suburb, CEC accreditation status, licence currency, customer satisfaction score, and brand access.

Our vetting criteria
  • Current CEC Designer & Installer accreditation
  • Valid state electrical contractor licence
  • Public liability insurance ≥ $5M
  • Minimum 2 years active in market
  • No unresolved complaints with state electrical authority
3
Your installer contacts you within 24 hours

Your matched installer calls or emails within 24 business hours. They'll ask to review your last 12 months of electricity bills, discuss your goals, and arrange a site visit if needed. This is obligation-free at every stage.

What your installer will provide
  • Full itemised, written quote
  • Federal rebate applied at point of sale
  • State rebate eligibility assessment
  • System sizing based on your actual usage
  • Estimated annual bill savings and payback period
4
You decide, entirely on your terms

Review the quote with full knowledge (our guides and tools are designed for exactly this moment). Accept it, decline it, or use our vetting checklist to dig deeper. No pressure, no follow-up harassment.

After you say yes
  • Installation typically takes 4–8 weeks (grid approval timelines vary)
  • Federal rebate deducted from your invoice, no claiming required
  • Grid connection managed by your installer
  • CEC certificate of compliance issued post-installation

Common questions

Is this really free for homeowners?
Yes, completely. We earn a referral fee from installers only when a job is completed, you pay nothing extra. Our matching service is funded by the installer side of the transaction.
Why only one installer and not four quotes?
Multiple quote services create a race-to-the-bottom where installers cut corners to win on price. We pre-select the best installer in your area so you get quality service, not a bidding war. If you want to compare, use our guides to benchmark the quote against market rates.
Am I obligated to proceed after requesting a match?
No. Requesting a match and receiving a quote creates zero obligation. You can say no at any point, including after a site visit. We ask installers to respect this unconditionally.
Will I receive follow-up marketing or calls from multiple companies?
No. Your details are shared with exactly one installer: your match. We don't sell data to third parties or other installers. See our Privacy Policy.

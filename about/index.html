About ReclaimPower

We built the platform we wished existed when we were buying a battery.

ReclaimPower is Australia's home battery matching platform. We connect homeowners with their area's single best CEC-accredited installer — vetted, licensed, no cowboys — and make sure every federal and state rebate is applied correctly.

Our Principles
🎯
One match, not four salespeople

We give each homeowner exactly one exclusive installer match. You get focused attention, not a bidding war between four companies all trying to upsell you.

🛡️
CEC-accredited only

Every installer in our network holds current CEC accreditation and a valid state electrical contractor licence. We verify this ourselves — not via a self-declaration form.

💰
Free for homeowners

Our service costs homeowners nothing. We earn a referral fee from installers when a job is completed — only after you're happy.

📚
Education first

We publish independent guides, comparison tools and a rebate calculator so you walk into any quote conversation fully informed.

🤝
Obligation-free

Requesting a match doesn't obligate you to buy. Get the quote, compare it against our guides, and decide without pressure.

🇦🇺
Australian-owned

ReclaimPower is owned and operated in Australia. We know the grid, the rebates and the market — from the inside.

Our Mission

Every Australian household should benefit from clean, affordable energy — without needing to be an expert to navigate it.

Australia has the highest rooftop solar penetration of any country on earth. But millions of solar households are still exporting their excess energy back to the grid for next to nothing — while buying it back at 10× the price at night.

Home batteries fix this. The federal government agrees — which is why the Cheaper Home Batteries Program is funded to 2030. Our job is to make sure homeowners can access it confidently, with a CEC-accredited installer they can trust.

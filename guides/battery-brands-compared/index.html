Tesla Powerwall 3
13.5kWh · All-in-one solar inverter + battery
★ Premium Pick

The Powerwall 3 integrates a solar inverter and battery into a single unit, with no separate inverter needed. High continuous power (11.5kW) means it can run almost anything in your home during an outage.

What does a home battery cost in Australia in 2026?

The short answer: a 10kWh battery system fully installed costs $7,000–$9,500 after the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program rebate (roughly 30% off). Before the rebate, expect $10,000–$14,000 for a 10kWh system depending on brand and installer.

The Cheaper Home Batteries Program, funded to 2030, provides a point-of-sale discount applied directly by your installer. You never have to claim it yourself.

What's included in the installed price?

A legitimate quote from a CEC-accredited installer should always include:

  • ✓Battery unit and mounting hardware
  • ✓Battery Management System (BMS)
  • ✓Inverter/charger (or hybrid inverter if new solar)
  • ✓Installation labour and electrical work
  • ✓Grid connection and metering (where required)
  • ✓Federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate applied at point of sale
  • ✓Monitoring app access
Watch Out For

Quotes that exclude installation, metering or grid connection costs. Always ask for a fully-installed, all-inclusive price, then compare apples to apples. Download our installer checklist for the exact questions to ask.
